# Nocturne

A rounded bar with rich status widgets and deep colors.

## Previews

Previews will be added with the Settings gallery.

## What it changes

A 40 pt bar of rounded capsules, with panels that hang from the bar. The bar supports top, bottom, left and right placement, and its contents scale with the thickness setting.

## Controls and commands

Use the bar buttons to open their panels. Change placement, thickness and widget options in **Settings > Styles & Widgets**.
