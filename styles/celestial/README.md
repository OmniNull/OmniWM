# Celestial

A vertical bar with spacious app and workspace tiles.

## Previews

Previews will be added with the Settings gallery.

## What it changes

A Caelestia-inspired 60 pt vertical bar with shaped workspaces, a status pill and a top-centre dashboard. It starts on the left and supports all four edges. The style brings its palette and the Rubik, Google Sans Flex and Material Symbols Rounded fonts.

## Controls and commands

Hover at the top centre to open the dashboard, with Dashboard, Media, Performance and Weather tabs. Use the bar buttons for the ordinary media, audio, calendar and system controls. Change placement, thickness and widget options in **Settings > Styles & Widgets**.
