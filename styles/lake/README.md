# Lake

A minimal bar and a customizable island for media, calendar, and more.

## Previews

Previews will be added with the Settings gallery.

## What it changes

A compact bar and a configurable Dynamic Island for media, agenda, weather, clocks and other components. It uses the shared palette you choose rather than a style-specific palette.

## Controls and commands

Turn on **Settings > Styles & Widgets > Island > Show Dynamic Island**. Hover for a preview, click to expand, swipe up to collapse, and use Escape or an outside press to dismiss. **Add Widget** adds island components; each has its own options.
